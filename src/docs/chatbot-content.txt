SECTION 11: STRONG CONVERSION QUESTIONS
Question 48
My hospital pharmacy is running, but I do not know whether it is truly profitable. Can Amretri check?
Answer
Yes. Pharmacy sales alone do not prove profitability.
Amretri can review purchase rates, margin, expiry, dead stock, discounts, billing leakage, stock value, vendor terms and staff productivity to understand the real pharmacy performance.
Question 49
My pharmacy sales are good, but cash flow is poor. Why?
Answer
This can happen due to excess stock, dead stock, slow-moving medicines, expiry loss, high purchase rates, credit sales, poor return control, billing leakage or weak inventory discipline.
Amretri can help review these areas and suggest corrective steps.
Question 50
My pharmacy depends on one person. Is this risky?
Answer
Yes. If one person controls purchase, stock, billing, vendor coordination and reporting, the pharmacy becomes person-dependent and risky.
Amretri can help create proper roles, reporting systems, SOPs and backup staffing.
Question 51
I am opening a new hospital. When should I start pharmacy planning?
Answer
Pharmacy planning should start before hospital launch, not after launch.
You should plan license requirements, layout, pharmacist staffing, initial stock, software, vendor list, SOPs, billing process, compliance records and emergency medicine availability before opening.
Amretri can help you plan this in advance.
