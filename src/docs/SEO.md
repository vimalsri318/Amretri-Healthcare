Amretri Healthcare Website Expansion Plan

Version: 1.0
Purpose: Website Structure, SEO Strategy & Lead Generation Roadmap

Overview

The current website has a strong foundation, but to generate consistent organic leads from Google, it should evolve from a company profile website into a lead-generation platform.

The website should target three primary business verticals:

Hospital Pharmacy Acquisition & Management
Pharmacist Supply & Staffing
Bulk Medicine Procurement Support

Every page, CTA, blog, and form should support one or more of these revenue streams.

Final Positioning Statement

Amretri Healthcare should not position itself as only a Hospital Pharmacy Acquisition Company.

Instead, the brand should become known as:

India's Trusted Hospital Pharmacy Operations Partner

Helping hospitals through:

Hospital Pharmacy Acquisition & Management
Professional Pharmacist Supply
Bulk Medicine Procurement
Inventory Optimization
Pharmacy Compliance & Audit
Pharmacy Consulting & Profit Improvement

This broader positioning significantly increases the number of high-intent search queries the website can rank for while creating multiple lead-generation channels from hospitals, healthcare institutions, and pharmacy operators across India.
